# P9 Non-Executed Off-Page Reference Readiness

## Decision

No external action is appropriate now. Mr. Copy’s website is a substantive reference resource, but the public Google Play URL, final commercial terms, and a product-access destination remain intentionally deferred. P9 created no account, listing, submission, outreach message, payment, campaign, or backlink request.

| Opportunity | Current eligibility evidence | P9 decision | Future prerequisite |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| AlternativeTo | The platform requires a verified email before new-app submission and does not accept Coming Soon, closed/private-beta, or invite-only software; a listing is reviewed before approval.[1] | **Hold.** A coming-soon Play CTA is not an accurate official app-store destination. | Confirmed public Play URL, verified Android availability, supported platform/license/description facts, and a separately approved single accurate application submission. |
| Product Hunt | Product Hunt describes a maker launch as a submission of a product URL from a personal account; makers should launch only when prepared and may not ask directly for upvotes.[2] | **Hold.** Do not create a personal account or launch while the store destination and commercial status are deferred. | Public product URL, launch-ready materials, owner-operated personal maker account, explicit approval, and a no-upvote-solicitation plan. |
| BetaList | BetaList looks for a relatively new technology startup with a distinct information-rich landing page and an accessible product path such as signup, login, download, or app-store link.[3] | **Hold.** The current site is visually substantive, but public product access and the final destination have not been confirmed. | Confirmed actual download/access path, release timing fit, and separate approval of one accurate submission. |

## Link-worthy resources retained

The existing Clipboard Manager, Floating Bubble, Link Previews, Privacy & Security, Android Clipboard Access, FAQ, and account-deletion documentation remain the only legitimate reference resources. Any future mention must describe Mr. Copy accurately as an Android local-reference and clipboard-management product. It must not use downloader, scraper, private-account access, cloud-sync, unsupported-platform, fabricated-review, paid-link, reciprocal-link, or mass-directory positioning.

## Deferred commercial and measurement facts

Google Play URL, price, plan, trial, geographic availability, rating, review, install, and promotion claims remain absent. Search Console Performance, Page indexing, HTTPS, and Core Web Vitals reports remain immature or unavailable for decision-making.[4] These facts must not be filled with estimates.
